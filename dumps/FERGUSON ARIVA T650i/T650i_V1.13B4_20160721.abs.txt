T650i_V1.13B4_20160721.abs  (from T650i_V1.13B4_20160721.zip)

Source: Ferguson's official download area, firmware for the Ariva T650i DVB-T receiver:
  https://ferguson-digital.eu/download/?dir=dvb-t%2Fseries_Ariva%2FAriva_T650i%2Ffirmware
The zip (2.94 MB, dated 2016-07-21 on the site) also holds a changelog (1.10B1 .. 1.13B4: new
YouTube / Vimeo / Dailymotion APIs, "Wybieram TV", timer and subtitle fixes) and upgrade guides in
EN / DE / PL; only the .abs image is kept here.

Device: Ferguson Ariva T650i, DVB-T HD receiver with Ethernet and web services (YouTube etc.),
ALi M3801.  This is a manufacturer UPDATE image, not a dump read from a box: a complete 8 MB flash
layout (bootloader included) but with an empty channel database.
Size 8388608 bytes, SHA-1 ea4978aabdaa1c890f01f8c297edde61deb409b3

Flash layout (chunk headers, 8 MB part): bootloader 0x000000 ("HTJ4" 63004-01047, Libcore
1.1.6@Auto_20100413, vic.wang Aug 25 2012), HDCPKey 0x01FE00, OTAloader 0x020000 (V1.13B5),
OTAparam 0x0FFE00, maincode 0x100000 (V1.13B5, LZMA-alone, 0x29693F -> 0xA7AC14 bytes; Libcore
8.1c.0@SDK4.0bd.8.7_20121127 = dump.bin's library, Application 1.0.0@SDK4.0bd.8.3_20120828),
radioback 0x700000, data1 0x71FF80, "T650i" 0x730000 (an MPEG still, the boot picture), data2
0x74FF80, defaultdb 0x770000, userdb 0x78FF80 (empty).  The upper 4 MB are reached the SDK way,
at 0xAFC00000 - 4 MB + offset (simulator.py _flash_offset).

In the simulator (2026-10-08): the bootloader identifies the flash as an 8 MB part (RES id 0x16,
its table's "16@56" entry), unpacks the maincode (~4-6 min) and prints "success!"; the front panel
is an FD650K ("PAN_FD650K", TM1650-compatible on GPIO 31 / 9) showing " On ", then "Strt" and
"Find".  The application rewrites three sectors in the upper 4 MB at start, resets its Ethernet
MAC (ETHERNET_MAC_0, 0xB802C000) and probes the PHY over MDIO (no PHY: reads 0xFFFF), then -- the
channel database being empty -- runs the first-install automatic search ("Przeszukiwanie auto",
Polish UI, Ferguson's black / orange skin): its progress screen from ~7 min, "nie znaleziono
kanału!" (no channel found) with a "tak" button at 100 % after ~18 min (217 GE commands).
