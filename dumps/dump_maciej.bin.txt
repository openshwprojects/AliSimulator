dump_maciej.bin

Source: elektroda.pl forum, thread "Jak skompilować i uruchomić własny firmware dla ALI M3801 i
innych układów z tunerów?", page 1, post #15 by maciej_333, 30 December 2015: attachment
"dump.bin" (4 MB), the flash of his Opticum STB HD N2 read through the bootloader's UART
console ("dump" command), which sends the 4 MB flash followed by a 4-byte CRC -- hence this
file's 4194308 bytes (the last 4 bytes, 5F E3 28 52, are that CRC, not flash contents).
Downloading attachments requires an elektroda account.
  https://www.elektroda.pl/rtvforum/topic4156384.html#21794256
  https://www.elektroda.com/rtvforum/topic4156384.html#21794256 (machine translation)

Device: Opticum STB HD N2 DVB-T set-top box, ALi M3801, SPI flash EN25Q32SB; TM1650 front panel
on GPIO 31 (SCL) / 9 (SDA).

Flash layout (chunk headers): bootloader 0x000000 (Libcore 1.1.6, jessie.wei, Fri Jun 15 2012),
HDCPKey 0x04FE00, maincode 0x050000 (name 406000000003002, version "M3801 DVBT", 2013-6-17,
LZMA), Radioback 0x290000, defaultdb 0x2A0000, userdb 0x2BFF80.  Application: Libcore
8.9.0@SDK4.0bd.8.9_20130409.

The attachment "opticum_hd_n2_upgraded.bin" of post #12 in the same thread (the box after the
firmware update maciej_333 found on chomikuj.pl, read with a programmer) is byte-identical to
the first 4 MB of this file, so it is not kept separately: the update installed the same
firmware the box already had.
