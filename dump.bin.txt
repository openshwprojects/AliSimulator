Device: Comsat TE 1050 HD DVB-T receiver, ALi M3801, main board MC6379-VER1.0, SPI flash
25Q32BSIG.  The application's system-information page names it "M3801", "MC6379-VER1.0",
"TE 1050 HD", "DVB-T HD V1.1.5", "Jan 29 2013".

Flash layout (chunk headers): bootloader 0x000000 (Libcore 1.1.6, vic.wang, Mon Nov 26 2012),
HDCPKey 0x01FE00 ("Demo M3801"), maincode 0x020000 (version "M3801 DVBT", 2013-2-1),
Radioback 0x320000, defaultdb 0x330000, userdb 0x34FF80.  Application: Libcore
8.1c.0@SDK4.0bd.8.7_20121127.
